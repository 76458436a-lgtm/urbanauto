about=Builds a map of built-up area for every year of a period (e.g. 2000–2025) from a city name or a polygon. The plugin downloads summer Landsat composites (optionally Sentinel-2) from Microsoft Planetary Computer, derives training labels from open products (GHSL, WSF, ESA WorldCover, JRC GSW, OpenStreetMap, Microsoft buildings), trains a gradient boosting / random forest model, detects the year built-up area appeared, and writes rasters, GeoPackage layers, a CSV series and an HTML report with data attribution. Unreliable yearly composites (low coverage, e.g. Landsat 7 SLC-off) are excluded and flagged. Optional labelled check points give kappa, overall accuracy and area with 95 % CI (Olofsson). The legacy index mode (NDBI, BSI, NDVI, MNDWI thresholds) is kept. Requires scikit-learn, scipy and requests in the QGIS Python (see README). Interface and reports are in Russian. Accuracy on new territories is not guaranteed and must be checked with independent points. / Карта застройки за каждый год по названию города или полигону; интерфейс на русском.
